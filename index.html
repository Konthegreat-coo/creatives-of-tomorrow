Who we are

A non-profit built on one simple belief

Creativity is one of the most powerful tools we have for building a more sustainable world.

Creatives of Tomorrow is a French non-profit association bringing people together around climate and environmental action — right here in Paris and across Île-de-France.

We work in three main ways: community cleanups that restore shared public spaces, educational workshops that make sustainability tangible and practical, and awareness campaigns that reach people where they already are.

Our mission

Turn creativity into climate action — making sustainability visible, tangible and shared across our city.

Our vision

A Paris where every neighbourhood, school and street corner is shaped by people who chose to act.

What we do

Our Actions

Three ways we turn belief into practice, all across Paris and Île-de-France.

Community Cleanups

We organise regular cleanups across Paris — parks, canals, riverbanks and public squares — restoring shared spaces one bag of waste at a time, together with local volunteers.

Educational Workshops

From schools to companies, we run hands-on sessions that make sustainability tangible and practical — turning abstract ideas about climate and waste into everyday habits.

Awareness Campaigns

We meet people where they already are — on the street, on campus, at local events — using creativity and design to make the case for a greener Paris impossible to ignore.
